Frequently Asked Questions

What will the whole thing cost me?

That depends on the task and the scope. As a guide: a clearance costs between 500 € (cellar) and 6.000 € (house) depending on the property, and an estate service 2.500–8.000 € per case. The key point: after the free viewing, you receive a fixed price in writing — and it stands exactly as written.

Is the viewing really free and without obligation?

Yes. We come round, take our time to look at everything and discuss what needs to be done. You then receive your fixed price in writing — and can decide in your own time. It only becomes binding with your agreement and the 50% deposit.

How does the value offset work?

During the viewing, we value reusable items — for example good furniture, electrical appliances, tools or e-bikes. We offset this value against your fixed price. So you don't pay twice for what is still good: it lowers your bill.

How quickly can you start?

Short-notice appointments are also possible. We agree the exact date together at the viewing. If things are particularly urgent, talk to us — for express appointments within 72 hours a surcharge may apply, which is then stated in the quote beforehand.

Do I have to be there during the clearance?

No. On request, we handle everything even if you cannot be there — with photo documentation so you can follow every step. We sort out personal items and important documents and hand them over to you; nothing simply disappears.

Which places do you cover?

Our area is Saarbrücken, Dillingen, Saarlouis, Wadgassen and approx. 50 km around. Your town isn't listed? Just ask — often more is possible than you might think.

Frequently asked questions about the sales service

What does the sales service cost me?

For vehicles: a 499 € fixed price for a sale value up to 15.000 €, 999 € above that — or, by agreement, 3–5% of the sale price. Individual items are sold for a 30% commission on the net proceeds. Everything is agreed in writing beforehand.

Could my car be sold below its value?

No. It is only sold at a price you have approved beforehand. We negotiate in your interest — without your approval, there is no sale.

Do I have to be there at viewings?

No. We answer all enquiries, organise and accompany the viewings, and handle the purchase contract and handover properly — the sale runs without your involvement.

Does this also work outside the Saarland?

Yes: vehicles are possible remotely across Germany. Viewings take place in the Saarland and within approx. 50 km.

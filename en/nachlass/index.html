Frequently asked questions about the estate service

I don't live locally — is it still possible?

Yes. On request, we handle everything even if you cannot be there — with photo documentation so you can follow every step. We sort out personal items and important documents and hand them over to you.

What happens to valuable pieces from the household?

We value reusable pieces during the viewing and offset their value against your fixed price — or we sell them for you. You decide what stays, what is sold and what is disposed of.

What does the estate service cost?

Depending on the size of the household and the effort involved, 2.500–8.000 € per case. Smaller partial tasks are possible at a lower price. You receive your binding fixed price in writing after the free viewing.

How discreet is the process?

Calm, respectful and unobtrusive — with one dedicated contact person. We discuss in detail beforehand what should happen with each item, and we stick to those agreements.

Frequently asked questions about clearance

What will a clearance cost me exactly?

The price range gives you a guide (cellar 500–900 €, house 2.500–6.000 €). We set your exact price after the free viewing — in writing and binding. The amount, access and effort decide where you land within the range.

What happens to things that are still good?

We value reusable items and offset their value against your fixed price. Other things are donated by agreement or disposed of with proof — you receive the receipts.

How quickly can the flat be empty?

Short-notice appointments are also possible. We agree the date at the viewing. For express appointments within 72 hours a surcharge may apply — it is stated in the quote beforehand.

Who is the handover to?

To whoever you wish: to you, the landlord, the buyer or the property management — broom-clean and on the agreed date. The balance is only paid at the handover.

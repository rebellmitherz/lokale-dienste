Typical measures

  • Bathroom: level-access shower, grab rails, non-slip floor, raised WC
  • Removing thresholds, widening doors, ramps
  • Coordinating a stairlift or handrails
  • Checking and removing lighting issues and trip hazards throughout the house

Frequently asked questions about the conversion

Does the care insurance fund really cover part of the costs?

If a care grade is in place, the care insurance fund subsidises home-improvement measures with up to 4.180 € per measure. Important: the application must be made before work begins — we put the documents together so that this works. Your care insurance fund decides on approval.

What does your coordination cost me?

Depending on the scope, our coordination fee is 990–2.400 € and is fixed in writing beforehand. The trades work is billed separately and transparently; you receive a written overview of everything beforehand.

Do you carry out the work yourselves?

We plan and coordinate: obtaining quotes, agreeing appointments, monitoring the work, joint inspection. The manual work is carried out by specialist firms — you still have only one contact person: us.

Which measures make sense in my case?

We look at that together during the free viewing: we walk through the house with you and agree the sensible measures — for example a level-access shower, grab rails, removing thresholds or better lighting.

Frequently asked questions about senior moves

What does a senior move cost?

Every move is different — a small flat or a whole house, near or further away. That is why we don't give flat-rate prices from a distance: after the free viewing, you receive a binding fixed price in writing. No hourly billing, no surcharges afterwards.

Do you also help with sorting and downsizing?

Yes, that is part of it: we sort together — what comes along, what stays in the family, what can be sold or donated. We sell reusable pieces for you or offset them against the price.

Will the furniture be back in place at the new home?

Yes. We pack, transport and set up your furniture at the new place. In the end, everything is in place — and the old home is handed over broom-clean.

Does everything have to happen in one day?

No. We work at your pace and with firm agreements — without time pressure. We agree the exact process together at the viewing.

What your everyday help takes care of

  • Shopping & errands: groceries, pharmacy, post office — from your list, or together with you
  • Household & cleaning: vacuuming, mopping, bathroom and kitchen, washing and ironing laundry
  • Accompaniment: to the doctor, to authorities, to the bank — and simply for a walk
  • Company: conversation, reading aloud, time together — against loneliness
  • Small helps: taking out the rubbish, watering plants, curtains, light tidying in the house

What we don't do — said honestly

No medical nursing care: no personal care, no medication, no wound care, no 24-hour care. That is the job of care services and nursing staff. If you need care, we will tell you openly — and we won't leave you alone with the question.

Frequently asked questions about everyday help

Does the same person always come?

Yes, that is the point: one regular contact person who knows you and your home. Only during holidays or illness does a substitute step in, by agreement.

What does the everyday help cost?

We agree the hourly rate with you in writing at the introductory meeting — it is fixed before the first visit begins. Billing is by actual time, via monthly invoice. There is no deposit.

Does the care insurance fund cover part of it?

The care insurance fund's relief amount (Entlastungsbetrag, 131 € per month) may only be billed by recognised providers — we are not currently recognised, so you pay privately. In return, you can claim 20% of our invoice as a household-related service against tax (up to 4.000 € per year).

Do I need a contract with a long term?

No. We agree the scope and schedule in writing, but without a long commitment: you can cancel monthly.

Is this also something for my parents — I don't live locally?

Yes, that is exactly what it is for: you commission us, we help your parents at home and, if you wish, keep you briefly up to date after each visit.

Frequently asked questions: getting a house ready for sale

Are you also estate agents who sell the house for me?

No. We are not estate agents and we do not find buyers. We prepare your house in the best possible way for the sale — clearance, deep cleaning, small repairs and photo documentation. The sale is handled by you or your agent.

What does it cost to have my house made ready for sale?

Depending on size, condition and the scope of the small repairs, 2.500–7.500 € per house. You receive your binding fixed price in writing after the free viewing — all the work as one package.

Who carries out the small repairs?

We coordinate the work and bring in specialist firms for the respective small jobs — for example painting, plumbing and electrical work. You still have only one contact person: us.

Do I get photos for my sales listing?

Yes. The before/after photo documentation is part of the package and is handy for your sales listing and the viewings.
